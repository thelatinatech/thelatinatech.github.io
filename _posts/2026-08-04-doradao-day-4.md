Do The Thing You Avoid... The GirlsWhoYap Fellowship is challenged me to do the things I've been avoiding. For this post, I've shared something personal, AND challenged myself by posting on Instagram!


For context: for well over a year, I had stopped posting on Instagram and on my blog. This was mainly due to burnout and my perfectionist mindset. I kept overthinking all that I wanted to do with my content, how I wanted to improve, how I wanted to grow my platforms... that eventually my expectations continued to rise while my energy and motivation continued to drop. 

TheLatinaTech blog has been a hobby of mine since 2020, and I've been wanting to have it grow since then. I've kept this hobby while I went through the pandemic, through major job changes, uprooting my life after massive personal changes to move to another state, starting and completing my masters' degree, and shifting from IT into cyber. Lots of change was happening that was stressful, and good in most cases, but my blog became to take a back seat. I felt I couldn't continue consistently, much less scale, in the way I had imagined. So I stopped.

I think that comparison also became a huge part of this pause with my content. I saw so many creators doing such cool and fun things online that I wished I could do the same things. I tried to keep up with the trends, with the next *new thing*, but it became unsustainable.

I've given it a lot of thought, and even part of participating in this fellowship, was a way that I wanted to encourage myself to build, to create, to post again.

I've tried changing my mindset in my time offline to move away from every post needs to be perfect. Away from the mindset of 'this isn't good enough', 'no one will read this', 'i poured so much work, effort and no one cares'. In a sense, that original mindset hurt because my purpose in creating the blog was to help others. To give insights into the unique world I didn't expect or dream to be a part of, but am fortunate enough that I did become part of it.

So to close out the "personal sharing" portion of this challenge. I'm armed with a more relaxed mindset. I don't care if no one like what I post. I like it, and I want to continue doing this for fun, rather than the self-imposed pressure that I thought I needed to have in order to succeed.

---

In addition to sharing something personal, I also challenged myself to post on Instagram again. I was a bit more hesitant, maybe even scared?, to do that since I have a slightly larger audience (of random followers, friends, and family) on there than on my blog. I tried not to think about "what would they think?", and just started creating a post in Canva. It felt nice to be able to be creative again. To get inspiration for the design of a post. I crafted out the posts, the captions, even chose some music and posted my return [here](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbpeOh7FM2b/?img_index=1), which summarized the first half of the GWY Fellowship sessions.

---

Who knows what's to come next in this program, but already I'm feeling challenged and I'm feeling positive that these challenges and session will continue to help me grow!
