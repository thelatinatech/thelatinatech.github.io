Breaking into cybersecurity can feel overwhelming, but getting started doesn't have to be. These three free, beginner-friendly certifications will help you build foundational skills, strengthen your resume, and take your first step toward a career in cyber.


Let's dive into the certifications that can help jump start your career in cybersecurity:

## Certified in Cybersecurity

The Certified in Cybersecurity credential is an ideal starting point for anyone new to the field. Created by (ISC)², a globally recognized organization, this certification covers foundational cybersecurity concepts to help you build a strong knowledge base.

**What You’ll Learn:**
- Security principles, such as confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA triad).
- Basics of network security, including firewalls, VPNs, and secure protocols.
- Risk management techniques, focusing on identifying, analyzing, and mitigating risks.

**Why It’s Great for Beginners:**

The course features self-paced modules and practice quizzes to ensure you grasp key concepts. It’s designed for those with no prior experience, so you’ll get a confidence boost while mastering the fundamentals.

## Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate

Offered through Coursera, the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate is a comprehensive program designed to equip you with practical, job-ready skills. Whether you’re exploring cybersecurity as a career or transitioning from another field, this certificate has something for you.

🌟 I've actually completed this one through [Latinas in Cyber](https://www.instagram.com/latinasincyber/)! While I am past the beginner phase of a cyber career, I think it was helpful in giving a broad overview of MANY different cyber topics and serving as a good refresher. I also liked that it provided the chance to apply what you learn in modules and labs, and gave examples of ways you could build up a cyber portfolio!

**What You’ll Learn:**

- Networking Fundamentals: Understand how data moves across the internet and how networks are secured.
- Cryptography Basics: Explore encryption techniques and their role in protecting sensitive data.
- Incident Response: Learn how to identify, respond to, and recover from cyber threats.

**Program Highlights:**

The course includes hands-on labs and assessments to give you real-world experience. Plus, it’s completely free to audit, making it accessible for anyone curious about cybersecurity

Find it here ➡️ [Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate](https://grow.google/certificates/cybersecurity/)


## Cisco Cybersecurity Essentials

Cisco’s Cybersecurity Essentials program offers an excellent introduction to the field, with a focus on real-world applications. Cisco, a leading name in networking, brings its expertise to this beginner-level certification to ensure you’re prepared for the essentials of cybersecurity.

**What You’ll Learn:**
- Cybersecurity Fundamentals: Get familiar with concepts like malware, phishing, and social engineering.
- Network Security: Learn how to secure networks and recognize vulnerabilities.
- Threat Analysis and Security Policies: Understand how to analyze threats and implement effective security policies to safeguard systems.

**Why You Should Choose This Certification:**

The course combines theoretical knowledge with practical exercises, helping you bridge the gap between learning and applying your skills. It’s perfect for anyone eager to explore a career in cybersecurity.

Find it here ➡️ [Cisco Cybersecurity Essentials](https://www.cisco.com/c/m/en_emear/campaigns/security/cybersecurity-essentials.html)
